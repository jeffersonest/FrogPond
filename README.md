# Frog Pond

A pixel-art arcade game about a frog on the bank of a pond. Every stage is generated from a seed,
so any stage can be shared as a code and played again by anyone.

- Catch flies, fireflies, beetles and dragonflies with a tongue you aim with the mouse.
- Survive wasp swarms, snakes and a boss every ten stages.
- Climb levels, spend points on agility, strength, tongue and your frog's ability, and win new
  frogs by beating their rivals.
- Play with friends online or on a local network: versus for up to six frogs in two teams, or
  survivor, where up to five frogs hold an island against waves of insects and a boss every ten
  waves.
- In English and Brazilian Portuguese.

**Download:** [jeffersonest.github.io/frog-pond-launcher](https://jeffersonest.github.io/frog-pond-launcher/)
for Windows and Linux, or straight from the
[latest release](https://github.com/jeffersonest/frog-pond-launcher/releases/latest).

packs and the download page live in

## Português

Um jogo de arcade em pixel art sobre um sapo na beira de um lago. Cada fase sai de uma semente,
então qualquer fase pode ser compartilhada como um código e jogada de novo por qualquer pessoa.
Pegue insetos com a língua, sobreviva a vespas, cobras e chefes, e jogue com amigos online ou na
rede local, em versus ou survivor.

**Baixar:** [jeffersonest.github.io/frog-pond-launcher](https://jeffersonest.github.io/frog-pond-launcher/),
para Windows e Linux.
